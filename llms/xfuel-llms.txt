# XFuel

> XFuel develops, patents, and deploys technologies that transform diverse waste into low-carbon drop-in transport fuels — marine (ISO 8217 DMA compliant MGO), road (EN590 diesel), and sustainable aviation fuel blends in development. Headquartered in Dublin, Ireland, with operations in Bunyola, Spain. Backed by Union Square Ventures (2022 climate fund investment). XFuel does not publish a public API or developer program.
